🌱 How to Use Rooted

A quick guide you can come back to anytime.

📖 1. Add Scripture or a prayer

Open Add New, give it a title, select your Bible translation, and paste or type the words. You can also change the translation and words later in Practice. For longer passages, put each verse or section on its own line.

🧠 2. Practice a little at a time

Open Practice, choose what you want to learn, and work through the sections. Try Hide / reveal words to test your memory.

🌳 3. Grow your tree

When you've truly memorized something, mark its sections complete. Visit My Tree to see your rewards: 🍃 leaf for a short verse, 🍎 apple for a longer passage, 🌟 golden apple for a big challenge, and ✝️ cross for a memorized prayer. Tap a reward label to review it.

✝️ Gospel Tree

Use the separate Gospel Tree to memorize verses about Creation, the Fall, Jesus, new life, and the invitation to follow Him. Tap Quick Gospel Guide when you need a reference while sharing the Gospel. Leaves, apples, and golden apples are earned by memorizing passages.

❤️ 4. Keep what matters close

Use Favorites for passages you want to revisit, and Journal to write down prayers and reflections.

💾 5. Keep your progress safe

Closing Rooted normally keeps your progress saved on this device. Before changing phones or clearing browser data, open Journal and tap Save My Progress. Keep the downloaded backup somewhere safe, like Google Drive. On your new phone, open Rooted and tap Restore My Progress.

There's no rush and no streak to keep alive. Grow at your own pace.

💾 Keep Your Progress Safe

Your prayers, Scriptures, pictures, journal, and tree rewards normally stay saved on this device, even after you close Rooted.

Downloads a private backup file. Save a copy to Google Drive, your computer, or a USB drive. Rooted does not automatically upload it.

Getting a new phone? Save a backup on your old phone, move the file to your new phone, then tap Restore My Progress here. Restoring replaces your current progress after confirmation.

Privacy: Backups may contain personal prayers and pictures. Keep them somewhere only you can access. Clearing website data or removing the app may erase progress if you have no backup.
